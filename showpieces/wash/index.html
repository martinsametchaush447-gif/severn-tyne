What it does to your surface

Pick a surface for the method, the expected finish and what needs care. Example advice: the surface and product need checking first.

Surface

Block paving
Usually responds well to cleaning. Pressure can displace jointing sand; re-sanding depends on the joints and surface, once dry.
Indian sandstone
Natural stone can scar under a concentrated jet. Usually a wide fan and low pressure are preferable; treatment and rinsing depend on the surface.
Tarmac
Too much pressure can strip the surface or loosen stones. Usually a gentle rinse is preferable; treatment depends on the surface and product.
Concrete
Tough, but older slabs etch and stripe. Test a corner, then a surface cleaner for an even finish.
Decking
Fan tip, along the grain, a good distance off. Close and hard, it furs the timber.
Render
Usually cleaned with low-pressure methods: forceful jets can drive water in or damage render. Treatment and rinsing depend on the surface and product.
Roof tiles
Forceful jets can drive water under tiles or wear their surface. Usually moss is removed carefully by hand; any treatment depends on the roof and product.
Conservatory
Forceful jets can damage seals or panels. Usually a soft brush and gentle rinse are preferable, depending on the surface.
