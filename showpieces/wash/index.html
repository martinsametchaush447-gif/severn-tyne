Right pressure for the surface

More pressure isn't more clean. Tap a surface to see how it should be done.

Surface

Block paving
Takes a proper clean, but the jet blows sand out of the joints, so it's re-sanded with kiln-dried sand once dry.
Indian sandstone
Soft natural stone scars and furs under a turbo nozzle. Treat the green, wide fan, low pressure, rinse gently.
Tarmac
Too much pressure strips the surface and loosens the stones. Treatment does the work; the rinse is gentle.
Concrete
Tough, but older slabs etch and stripe. Test a corner, then a surface cleaner for an even finish.
Decking
Fan tip, along the grain, a good distance off. Close and hard, it furs the timber.
Render
Never jetted: it forces water in and can crack and blow the render. A biocide kills the green, then a low-pressure rinse.
Roof tiles
A jet drives water under the tiles and wears their surface. Moss comes off by hand, then a biocide helps slow regrowth.
Conservatory
Jets break seals and mark the panels. Low pressure, a soft brush and a gentle rinse.
