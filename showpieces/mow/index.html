What does the garden need this month?

Three useful jobs for a UK garden. Let the weather and your plants decide the timing.

Month

A little care, at the right time.

January
  • Keep off frozen or waterlogged grass.
  • Clear leaves from paths when safe.
  • Check tree ties for rubbing.
February
  • Clean and sharpen garden tools.
  • Weed borders when the soil is workable.
  • Check fences after strong winds.
March
  • Start mowing if grass is growing and dry.
  • Tidy border edges.
  • Mulch bare soil while it is moist.
April
  • Mow little and often as growth picks up.
  • Weed before plants set seed.
  • Water new planting in dry spells.
May
  • Keep a patch of long grass for wildlife.
  • Water new plants at the roots.
  • Check hedges for active nests before cutting.
June
  • Mow to suit growth and ground conditions.
  • Hoe weeds on a dry day.
  • Trim suitable hedges after checking for nests.
July
  • Raise the mower height in dry weather.
  • Water pots when the compost needs it.
  • Deadhead spent flowers where appropriate.
August
  • Leave drought-stressed lawns longer.
  • Keep new planting watered.
  • Weed borders before weeds shed seed.
September
  • Repair bare lawn patches in mild weather.
  • Divide suitable established perennials.
  • Keep leaves off the lawn.
October
  • Collect leaves for leaf mould.
  • Plant suitable shrubs in workable soil.
  • Mow only while growth and conditions allow.
November
  • Clear fallen leaves from the lawn.
  • Keep off soggy or frozen grass.
  • Check stakes and ties before winter winds.
December
  • Clean tools ready for next season.
  • Keep paths clear of slippery leaves.
  • Leave some seed heads for wildlife.

Check for active nests before hedge work. Avoid mowing wet, frozen or drought-stressed grass.
