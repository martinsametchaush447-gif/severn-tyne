Right finish for the room

Pick the room. The sheen is only part of the choice: the paint must suit the surface and the wear.

Room or surface
MattDurable mattEggshellSatinGloss

Kitchen
Choose a washable formula suitable for cooking splashes.
Bathroom
A suitable moisture-resistant formula helps in a steamy room; ventilation still matters.
Hallway
A washable finish makes everyday scuffs easier to deal with.
Bedroom
A low-sheen finish suits walls with less wear.
Woodwork
A mid-sheen finish suits doors and skirting; gloss is a shinier alternative.
Ceiling
Low sheen helps soften the appearance of small surface imperfections.
