A finish for every room

Pick the room. The sheen is only part of the choice: the paint must suit the surface and the wear.

Room or surface
MattDurable mattEggshellSatinGloss

Kitchen
Choose a washable formula suitable for cooking splashes.
Bathroom
Eggshell has a gentle sheen. Choose a product rated for the moisture and surface; ventilation still matters.
Hallway
A washable finish makes everyday scuffs easier to deal with.
Bedroom
Matt softens reflected light on bedroom walls with less wear; it is not always washable.
Woodwork
Satin gives doors and skirting a mid-sheen, wipeable finish. Preparation and the right primer matter.
Ceiling
Low sheen helps soften the appearance of small surface imperfections.
