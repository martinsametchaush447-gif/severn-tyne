      Questions

      How fast can my site be live?

      We agree a date with you before we start.

      Who owns the domain?

      Your domain is registered for your business and you can take it with you.

      What does the monthly plan cover?

      Hosting, your local phone number, updates when you ask for them, and new posts and short videos each month.

      Can I change things later?

      Yes. Send us a message with what you want changed, like new prices, a new photo or holiday hours, and we update the site for you.

      I already have a website. Can you help?

      Yes. We can rebuild it and move it across to your existing address, so customers still find you in the same place.

      Do I need to supply photos?

      Your own photos work best, and phone photos are fine. If you have none, we can start with what is on your Google listing, with your permission, and add better ones later.
